Who we are

We came up in vanilla on Shattered Hand, back when a group quest in Hillsbrad took a whole evening and nobody minded. The Myrmidons are the same crowd, still here, still friendly.

Our first priority is a good time. Level with people you like, run dungeons that actually get finished, and build toward raiding at a pace that suits the whole guild.

"No pressure, no drama. Bring a good attitude and a few spare bags."
— Ruwa, Warchief

The road ahead

Leveling together

Where we are now. Questing groups, guild chat, and help with any tough elite.

Dungeon runs

Weekly group nights through the 5-man content, with gear sharing for alts and newcomers.

Raid nights

Once we have the numbers, we start casual raids. Two nights a week, with plenty of breaks.
