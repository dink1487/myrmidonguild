A guild with history

Good company, since vanilla

We came up in vanilla on Shattered Hand, back when a group quest in Hillsbrad took a whole evening and nobody minded. The Myrmidons are the same crowd, still here, still friendly.

Our first priority is a good time. Level with people you like, run dungeons that actually get finished, and build toward raiding at a pace that suits the whole guild.

Good people make a good guild

Code of Conduct

Real life is stressful enough. Guild time is for relaxing, playing, and enjoying good company.

Respect each other

Be kind in chat, voice, groups, and raids. Keep jokes friendly, and stop when someone says they’re not enjoying them.

No racism or hate

Racism, slurs, hate speech, harassment, and discrimination have no place here. Treat people with respect, whatever their background or identity.

Leave politics at the door

Keep guild chat and Discord free of political arguments and divisive real-world debates. We’re here to chill and enjoy the game together.

Real life comes first

There’s no pressure to play, level, or raid on a schedule. Be patient with each other, and give people room to step away when life gets busy.

If something feels off, reach out to an officer. We can help reset the conversation and keep the guild welcoming for everyone.
